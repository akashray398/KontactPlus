# Kontact++ Store Asset Plan

This document outlines the visual assets required for the Google Play Store listing.

## 1. App Icon
- **Size**: 512 × 512 pixels
- **Format**: PNG
- **Design**: Clean "K++" logo using brand violet and electric cyan. Adaptive icon support included.

## 2. Feature Graphic
- **Size**: 1024 × 500 pixels
- **Format**: PNG or JPEG
- **Content**: Brand logo, product name, and the tagline "Connect smarter. Remember better." on a professional gradient background.

## 4. Screenshot Specifications
- **Aspect Ratio**: 16:9 or 18:9 (e.g. 1080 × 1920 or 1080 × 2160)
- **Status Bar**: Cleaned (No notifications, full battery, full signal).
- **Navigation Bar**: Visible or gesture-hint based on current device trends.
