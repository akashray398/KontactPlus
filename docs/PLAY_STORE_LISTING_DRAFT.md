# Play Store Listing Draft: Kontact++

## App Title
Kontact++: Private Dialer & Assistant

## Short Description
Smart dialer and relationship manager. Private notes, reminders, and AI help.

## Full Description
**Connect Smarter. Remember Better.**

Kontact++ is a privacy-first contacts manager and relationship assistant designed to help you stay in touch with the people who matter most.

### 📞 A Better Dialer
Make Kontact++ your default phone application to handle your calls with a beautiful, modern interface. 
- Fast Keypad: Quickly dial numbers or search your contacts.
- Recents: Access your call history locally.
- In-Call Controls: Mute, speaker, and hold with ease.

### 🧠 Relationship Assistant
Never forget a detail again. Kontact++ gives you a private space to track the context of your relationships.
- Private Notes: Jot down personal details, gift ideas, or conversation summaries.
- Important Dates: Track birthdays and anniversaries with local notifications.
- Follow-up Reminders: Set schedules to remind you to reach out.
- Connection Insights: Get on-device suggestions for unreturned missed calls or due follow-ups.

### 🔒 Privacy First
Your data belongs to you.
- No Automatic Uploads: Your contacts and call logs are processed locally and never leave your device.
- Local Storage: Notes and reminders are stored in a local database, excluded from cloud backups.
- Optional AI assistance: Use our optional AI writing tools to draft polite follow-ups. Nothing is sent to the cloud until you review and approve the exact text.
- No Analytics: We don't track your app usage or listen to your calls.

### ✍️ Smart Writing Tools (Optional)
- Draft follow-ups after important calls.
- Generate warm birthday greetings.
- Summarize long notes.
- *Everything requires your explicit approval before it is sent for processing.*

Kontact++ is open-source and committed to total transparency. Connect smarter today!
